Import or paste CSV, TSV or JSON, choose a graph type, and adjust the colours, axes, labels and layout in a live preview. Export stills as SVG or PNG, or build an animation and save it as MP4 or GIF.

Everything runs in your browser, with charts saved locally. Built out of a love for minimalist graphs and a desire to make creating images for this blog less cumbersome.
